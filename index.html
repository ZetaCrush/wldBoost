🔥 About WorldCoin Boost Chat
Burn real value. Get a boosted response.

When a question matters enough that you want to do everything possible to get the best answer — burn WorldCoin on Ethereum mainnet to signal your commitment. Most AI interactions are free and disposable. This one isn't. Sam Altman is the founder of both ChatGPT and Worldcoin, allowing burning of his coin to serve as a potential revenue source.

How It Works

1. Connect & Sign — Connect your Ethereum wallet and sign a challenge proving you control the address.

2. Burn WLD — 95% of your WLD is sent to the dead address 0x000…dEaD on Ethereum mainnet, permanently destroyed. A 5% platform fee is applied via smart contract.

3. On-Chain Verification — GPT-5.5 independently looks up your transaction on Etherscan via web search. It confirms the WLD contract, burn address, amount, and that the sender matches your signed wallet before responding. The boost is cryptographically verifiable, not just claimed.

Why It Works

The model is instructed to treat a verified burn as evidence of serious intent and respond with greater depth and thoroughness. The more WLD burned, the stronger the signal.
