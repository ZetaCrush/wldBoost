How It Works

1. Connect & Sign — Connect your Ethereum wallet and sign a challenge proving you control the address.

2. Burn WLD — Transfer WLD to the dead address 0x000…dEaD on Ethereum mainnet. The tokens are permanently destroyed.

3. On-Chain Verification — GPT-5.5 independently looks up your transaction on Etherscan via web search. It confirms the WLD contract, burn address, amount, and that the sender matches your signed wallet before responding. The boost is cryptographically verifiable, not just claimed.

Why It Works

The model is instructed to treat a verified burn as evidence of serious intent and respond with greater depth and thoroughness. The more WLD burned, the stronger the signal.
